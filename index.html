About

I am a PhD student working on image and video generation and editing. I want diffusion and flow models to be controllable, and I want to understand what video models know about the world, so they can be used as world models and for reasoning.

My recent work is on joint audio-video diffusion models. Soundwich splits the generated audio into stems (speech, music, effects, ambience) that can be edited independently. Sound Sparks Motion edits motion at test time, without retraining the model.

Earlier I worked on 3D and 4D reconstruction at ETH Zurich and INSAIT, and on robot manipulation at the University of Toronto. I did my BSc at Sharif University of Technology, and worked at Tapsi on ETA and navigation models.

Research

Control in image and video generation and editing

Precise control over image, video and audio-video diffusion models without retraining them: tuning the conditioning at test time, and splitting what a model generates into parts that can be edited separately.

Soundwich · Sound Sparks Motion

Video models as world models and reasoners

Video diffusion models appear to learn physics, geometry and cause and effect. I want to understand how to use them as simulators of the world, and as models that can reason.

Ongoing

3D and 4D scene representations

Dynamic scene representations: Gaussian Splatting and implicit fields that handle large topological change, and compression that keeps visual fidelity.

N4DE · Confident Splatting · mlx3D

Soundwich: Video Generation with Layered and Controllable Audio

Zhuo Ning, AmirHossein Naghi Razlighi, Sagi Polaczek, Daniel Cohen-Or, Ali Mahdavi-Amiri

Decomposes the audio of joint audio-video generative models into independently controllable stems (speech, music, sound effects and ambience) tied together by a shared scene representation. Each stem can be adjusted, muted, replaced or remixed while staying in sync with the video.

arXiv preprint · under review at ICLR 2027

Sound Sparks Motion: Audio and Text Tuning for Video Editing

AmirHossein Naghi Razlighi, Aryan Mikaeili, Ali Mahdavi-Amiri, Daniel Cohen-Or, Yiorgos Chrysanthou

A test-time tuning framework for motion-focused video editing inside audio-visual generative models. Instead of retraining, we tune two lightweight variables, an audio latent and a text-conditioning residual, guided by vision-language model feedback. The learned controls transfer across videos.

Preprint, 2025

Confident Splatting: Confidence-Based Compression of 3D Gaussian Splatting via Learnable Beta Distributions

AmirHossein Naghi Razlighi, Elaheh Badali Golezani, Shohreh Kasaei

Lossy 3DGS compression with learnable Beta-distribution confidence scores. Low-confidence splats are pruned while visual fidelity is preserved. The method is architecture-agnostic and introduces average confidence as a scene-quality metric.

arXivProject pageCode

N4DE: Neural 4D Evolution under Large Topological Changes from 2D Images

AmirHossein Naghi Razlighi, Tiago Novello, Asen Nachkov, Thomas Probst, Danda Paudel

A new architecture and temporal-consistency technique for 4D reconstruction under large topological changes, with a Gaussian Splatting rendering scheme and a geometry-appearance disentanglement learned directly from RGB images.

MARS: Multi-task Action Prediction for Robot Manipulation based on Scene Representation

Haoping Xu, Richard Hanxu*, AmirHossein Naghi Razlighi*, Alan Aspuru-Guzik, Florian Shkurti, Animesh Garg

Uses Scene Representation Transformers to render novel views from RGB observations, then predicts end-effector poses and gripper states from language-conditioned keyframe demonstrations.
